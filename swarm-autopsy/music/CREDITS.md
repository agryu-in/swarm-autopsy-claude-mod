# Music credits

Every track here is lofi and CC0 (a public-domain dedication), checked on its own page. Each was converted for the mod
with ffmpeg: mono MP3, 96 kbps, 44.1 kHz, loudness-matched to about −23 LUFS (two-pass `loudnorm`). Every track not made
as a seamless loop (all but Lofi Hip Hop Loop) has a 1.5 s fade-in and a 3 s fade-out, so it comes round again softly.
Nothing else was changed.
